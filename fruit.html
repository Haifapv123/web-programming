A fruit is the seed-bearing structure in flowering plants that develops from the ovary after flowering.Definition and MeaningBotanical definition: The ripened ovary of a flowering plant that encloses one or more seeds. This includes sweet produce like apples and bananas, as well as botanical fruits often treated as vegetables like tomatoes, cucumbers, and pea pods.Culinary definition: Fleshy, edible plant structures that are typically sweet or tart and eaten raw or in desserts.Types of FruitsSimple fruits: Formed from a single ovary of one flower, such as stone fruits (peaches), pomes (apples), and berries (grapes).Aggregate fruits: Formed from multiple ovaries in a single flower, such as strawberries and raspberries.Multiple fruits: Formed from the fusion of ovaries from multiple separate flowers, such as pineapples and blackberries.Dry fruits: Structures like nuts, legume pods, and grains that are not fleshy.Health BenefitsRich sources of essential dietary fiber, vitamins (like Vitamin C), and antioxidants.Regular consumption supports heart health, improves digestion, and helps lower the risk of chronic conditions.
