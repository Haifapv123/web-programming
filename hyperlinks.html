FESTIVALS OF INDIA

India celebrates hundreds of vibrant festivals all year round, rooted in diverse faiths, seasons, and regional traditions. They generally fall into major religious and national

1.ONAM

Onam the national festival of Keralam.

2.HOLI

Holi is a major Hindu festival of colours, love and spring.

3.DIWALI

Dipavali (IAST: Dīpāvalī)commonly known as Diwali is the Hindu festival of lights.
